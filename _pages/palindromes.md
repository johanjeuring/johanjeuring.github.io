Through the years I've been working on palindromes:
- [software for finding palindromes](https://hackage.haskell.org/package/palindromes)
- I wrote a paper about an [algorithm for finding palindromes](https://webspace.science.uu.nl/~jeuri101/homepage/Publications/algorithmicapalindromes.pdf), the various [variants of the problem](https://ics-archive.science.uu.nl/research/techreps/repo/CS-2013/2013-016.pdf), and the [history of finding palindromes in computer science](https://ics-archive.science.uu.nl/research/techreps/repo/CS-2013/2013-008.pdf)
- a [blog](https://finding-palindromes.blogspot.com) about all kinds of aspects related to palindromes
- on my [finding palindromes website](https://www.finding-palindromes.net/) you can find all kinds of palindromes in texts you upload. I will further improve the website in the coming period; let me know if you have any requests or ideas
